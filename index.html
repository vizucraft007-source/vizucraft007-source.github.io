Kits

What's inside every kit

Built for Unity 6 and TextMeshPro.

Ready prefabs

Buttons, panels, tabs, toggles, sliders, popups and full screens you can drop into a scene.

9-sliced sprites

High-resolution art with slicing already set up, so panels scale to any size without stretching.

Animated components

Button presses, popups, tab switches, counters and reward reveals — scripts included.

Demo scenes

Open a scene and click through the whole interface to see how everything fits together.

SVG sources

Vector sources for every sprite, so you can recolor or adapt the art to your game.

Clear licensing

Fonts and icons under open licenses, listed in a Third-Party Notices file.
